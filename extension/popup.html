Paste the secret key of your TU TOTP token, or the otpauth:// link from its QR code.

Where to find it: on the TU login page open 2FA-Management, add a new TOTP token and copy the key shown next to its QR code. Scan the same QR code with your phone app too, so both keep working.

The key is stored only in this Chrome profile (not encrypted). Anyone with access to this browser could generate your codes.
